Critics Consensus

A candy-colored ode to friendship and Filipino folklore, Forgotten Island bursts at the seams with imagination while never losing sight of the compelling relationship at its core.

Audience Says

A vibrant and emotionally rewarding adventure that celebrates Filipino culture, Forgotten Island is as unforgettable as the friendship at its heart.
